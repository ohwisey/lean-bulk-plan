Help me make a simple workout plan that fits my life.

Ask me a few questions at a time. Wait for my answers before writing the plan.

Find out:
• What I want to achieve and by when.
• My age and how long I've been lifting.
• How many days I can train, how much time I have, and what equipment I can use.
• My current workouts, recent weights and reps, and other exercise I do.
• What I enjoy, what isn't improving, how I sleep, and how tired I feel.
• Any pain, injuries or limits I need to work around.

Then give me:
• A plan for the next 4–6 weeks that fits my schedule.
• Each day's exercises in order, warm-up, sets, reps and rest times. Explain how hard each set should feel.
• A way to choose starting weights with good form. Don't guess weights or copy Luke's numbers.
• Simple rules for adding reps or weight, missing a workout, or feeling worn out.
• Other exercises I can use if one doesn't suit me.
• A short workout log I can save in my notes.

Ask if I want help with food. If I do, ask about my size, weight trend, goal and eating habits first. Explain any estimates. Don't automatically put me on a bulk or a strict diet.

Use plain words. Explain why the plan fits me. Be honest about what you don't know and link reliable sources for factual claims. Don't promise results or tell me to push through pain. Say when I need a trainer, clinician or dietitian.

Start by asking your first questions. We'll review the plan after two weeks.
